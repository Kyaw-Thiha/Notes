# Topology
[[Topology|Topology]] is the study of properties of geometric objects that are preserved under continuous deformations such as stretching, twisting, crumpling and bending.

![150](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/2/24/Spot_the_cow.gif?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=imageinfo&utm_content=thumbnail_unscaled)

That is to say, without closing holes, opening holes, tearing, gluing and passing through itself.

---
## Topological Equivalence 
Two [[Degrees of Freedom(DOF)|spaces]] are [[Topology|topologically equivalent]] if one can be smooth deformed to the other without cutting and gluing. 
![150](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/2/26/Mug_and_Torus_morph.gif?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=index&utm_content=original)

---
### Topologically Distinct
Some topologically distinct one-dimensional spaces are
- a circle
- a line, and
- a closed interval of a line

Some topologically distinct two-dimensional spaces include
- the plane
- the surface of a sphere
- the surface of a torus, and
- the surface of a cylinder
