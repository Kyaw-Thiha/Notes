## Tangent Function
Tangent function can be described as
$$
\begin{align*}
&y = x \tan\theta \\[1em]
&\tan\theta = \frac{y}{x} \\[1em]
&\theta = \tan^{-1}\frac{y}{x} \qquad \tan^{-1} \sim \operatorname{atan} \sim \arctan
\end{align*}
$$

## Two-Argument Arctangent
$\theta = \operatorname{atan2}(y,x)$ is the two-argument arctangent which returns the angle between the positive $x\text{-axis}$ and the point $(x,y)$.
$$
\theta = \operatorname{atan2}(y,x) \in (-\pi, \pi]
$$
