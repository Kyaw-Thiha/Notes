## Question-1
Derive the complete forward kinematics of a planar 2R manipulator shown in Figure 1 using Product of Exponentials (PoE) formula in **space form**. How do you obtain the expression for the $x_{b}$ and $y_{b}$ coordinate of the end-effector?

### Solution
Deriving the matrix $M$, we get
$$
M = \begin{bmatrix}
1 & 0 & l_{1} + l_{2} \\[6pt]
0 & 1 & 0 \\[6pt]
0 & 0 & 1
\end{bmatrix}
$$
