# Sensor
#robotics/sensor 
A [[Sensor|sensor]] is a device which measures a specific physical quantity.

![Sensor|400](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0559/1970/6265/files/What_is_a_Sensor.webp?v=1744357084)

---
## Properties of Good Sensors
- Sensitive to physical quantity being measured
- Insensitive to other physical quantity encountered during use.
- Doesn't change value of physical quantity being measured by significant amount.
- Has a simple response curve like $r(t) = f(t) =at + b$.

---
## Sensor Types
The `sensor` can be electronic, mechanical, chemical or something else.

![Different Sensors|300](https://www.hackatronic.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/Types-of-Sensors-.webp)

There are many types of sensors to collect different data.
