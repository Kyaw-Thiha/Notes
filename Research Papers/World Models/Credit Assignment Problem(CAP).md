# Credit Assignment Problem
[[Credit Assignment Problem(CAP)|Credit assignment problem]] refers to the problem of measuring the influence and impact of an action taken by an agent on future rewards.

![250](https://www.frontiersin.org/files/Articles/314896/xml-images/fnins-12-00182-g0001.webp)

---
## Problems
> **Delayed Rewards**
> Consequences of actions may not be apparent till later.

> **Sparse Rewards**
> Agent may receive feedback only at the end of an episode.

> **Long Horizons**
> Small errors in estimating an action's contribution can accumulate.

> **Stochastic Environment**
> Outcome may be partly due to environment dynamics or luck.

> **Multiple Agents**
> A shared team does not directly reveal each agent's contribution.
