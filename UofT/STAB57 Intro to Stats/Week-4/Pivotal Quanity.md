# Pivotal Quantity
A [[Pivotal Quanity|pivotal quantity]] is a random variable defined in terms of the sample $X_{1}, X_{2}, \dots, X_{n}$.
- It involves unknown parameters in its expression.
- But distribution of this random variable does not depend on the parameters.

---
## Examples
- $\frac{\bar{X}-\mu_{0}}{\sigma/\sqrt{ n }} \sim \mathbb{N}(0,1)$
- $\frac{\bar{X} - \mu}{S/\sqrt{ n }} \sim t_{(n-1)}$
- $\frac{(n-1)S^{2}}{\sigma^{2}} \sim \mathcal{X}^{2}_{(n-1)}$
