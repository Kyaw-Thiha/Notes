# Week-4
Suppose $X_{1}, \dots, X_{n} \sim \mathbb{N}(\mu, \sigma^{2})$.  Then, we have
- $\bar{X} \sim \mathbb{N}\left( \mu, \frac{\sigma^{2}}{n} \right)$ $\implies$ $\frac{\bar{X}-\mu}{\sigma/\sqrt{ n }} \sim \mathbb{N}(0,1)$
- $\frac{(n-1)S^{2}}{\sigma^{2}} \sim \mathcal{X}^{2}_{(n-1)}$
- $\frac{\bar{X} - \mu}{S/\sqrt{ n }} \sim t_{(n-1)}$

This week is about interval estimation.
