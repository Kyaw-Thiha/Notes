## Definitions
**Population**: The collection of everything.

**Sample**: Subset of the population.

**Parameter**: A summary of the population.
- fixed
- unknown
- constant

**Statistics**: A summary of the sample.

---
## Example
- $x_{i} = \text{height}$
- Population: All of UofT Students.
- Parameter: Average height $(\mu)$
- Statistics: Average height of the sample.
