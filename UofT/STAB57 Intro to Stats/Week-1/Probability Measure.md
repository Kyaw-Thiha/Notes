# Probability Measure
The [[Probability Measure|probability measure]] $P$ for each event $A$ defined on sample space $\Omega$ satisfies the following properties:
- $P(A)$ is non-negative and $0 \leq P(A) \leq 1$
- $P(A)=0$ when $A$ is empty
- $P(A)=1$ when $A$ is the entire sample space $\Omega$
- $P$ is (countably) additive

$$
P[A_{1} \cup A_{2} \cup \dots A_{n}]
= \sum ^{n}_{i=1} P[A_{i}]
$$
