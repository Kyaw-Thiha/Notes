# Variance
[[Variance]] can be thought of as radius of the circle.
$$
V[X] = E[(X - E[X])^{2}] = E[X^{2}] - (E[X])^{2}
$$

---
## Properties
- $V[aX + bY + c] = a^{2}V[X] + b^{2}V[Y]$, where
	- $a, \ b, \ c$ are constants
	- and $X$ and $Y$ are independent
	- or $cov[X,Y] = 0$(since independance $\implies$ $cov=0$)
- $V[c] = 0$, where $c$ is a constant
