# Law of Large Numbers(LLN)
[[Law of Large Numbers(LLN)]] means sample mean approaches the population mean as the sample size increases.

Let $X_{1}, \ X_{2},  \dots , \ X_{n}$ be a sequence of independent random variables. Let $X_{n} = \frac{1}{n} \sum ^{n}_{i=1} X_{i}$. Then,
$$
X_{n} \xrightarrow{p} \mu
\quad \text{ as } n\to \infty
$$
In easier temrs, we can define as
$$
\frac{1}{n} \sum x_{i} \xrightarrow{p} E[X_{i}]
$$

---
## Formal Definition
Formally, we can define as
$$
\lim_{ n \to \infty } P[|X_{n} - \mu| > \epsilon] = 0
$$
