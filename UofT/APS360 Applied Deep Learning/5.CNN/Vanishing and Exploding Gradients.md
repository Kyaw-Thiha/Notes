# Vanishing and Exploding Gradients
[[Vanishing and Exploding Gradients|Vanishing and exploding gradients]] are mathematical issues in [[Neural Network|deep learning]] where error shrink to zero or grow infinitely large during [[Backpropagation|backpropagation]].
