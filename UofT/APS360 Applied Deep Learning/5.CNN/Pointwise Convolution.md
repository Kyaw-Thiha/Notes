# Pointwise Convolution $(1 \times 1)$
[[Pointwise Convolution|Pointwise convolution]] are $1 \times 1$ [[Convolution Layer|convolution operation]] used to mix information across the channel.

![400](https://global.discourse-cdn.com/dlai/original/2X/0/012816e3d8ab7a27f8a728c9e42aee97b943e153.jpeg)

They can be thought of as pixel-wise linear transformations.

---
They can be used to map [[Convolutional Neural Network (CNN)|CNN features]] into lower or higher dimensional space. 

![500](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/354079728/figure/fig2/AS:1060130711949312@1629766266227/Difference-between-regular-depthwise-and-pointwise-convolution.png)

Hence, they are good for learning compact representation.
