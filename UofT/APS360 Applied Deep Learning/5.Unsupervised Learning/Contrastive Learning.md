# Contrastive Learning
[[Contrastive Learning|Contrastive learning]] is a [[Self-Supervised Learning|self-supervised technique]] where 
- positive pairs are pulled together in the vector space, and
- negative pairs are pushed further apart.

![300](https://learnopencv.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/Contrastive-Learning-SimCLR-and-BYOL-featured-image.gif)


It essentially 
- contrast pairs of positive and negative samples
- compute the [[Loss Function|loss]] in the embedding space
- compress relevant information.

![image|400](https://notes-media.kthiha.com/Constrastive-Learning/0168e45d52ca72421c2342601f90a3e8.png)

It should be noted that [[Contrastive Learning|contrastive learning]] requires a lot of negative examples.
